Ways of Working

  • Map before designing. On both the ERP and Fieldtrack, the first deliverable was an understanding of the existing process, not a schema. Designs that skip this get rebuilt.
  • Write the contract down first. Data model and API contract agreed with consuming teams before build starts, then documented in Postman and kept current.
  • Take the feedback early. Designs get challenged before build, and the cheapest moment to find a simpler approach is while nothing has been written yet.
  • Ship with the pipeline and monitoring already in place. CI/CD, artifact management, and dashboards for health and business metrics are part of the release, not a follow-up ticket.
