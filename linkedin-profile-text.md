## 2. About

```
I build and design backend services. Right now that means the Node.js services
behind OLX Indonesia's marketplace, admin, and dealer applications at Astra
Digital Mobil: listings, car inspections, auctions, and the virtual-account flow
dealers use to settle vehicles they win.

Eight years in, most of my work has settled into the same shape. Take a
requirement, decide where the service and data boundaries go, agree the API
contract with whoever has to consume it, then build it and make sure the pipeline
and the dashboards exist before it ships. The design side is mine end to end:
service boundaries, data models, and the API contracts the teams consuming them
work against.

Just before OLX I spent three months freelancing for the agrarian and spatial
planning ministry, building the national land bank monitoring dashboard its
Direktorat Bank Tanah now works from, replacing a manual Excel recap. The four years before that were at a telco software house, where small
teams meant I was the technical owner on a product from the first client
conversation through to production: network monitoring (Netsight), a self-service dashboard platform
(Realviux), a capacity planning dashboard for Telkomsel, and an internal ERP. Two
of those started by mapping how people actually worked before any code was
written, and I would recommend that to anyone building internal tools. Earlier,
agency work meant scoping, estimating, and handing over client systems, including
a dock management system for DHL Supply Chain.

Day to day: Node.js, Nest.js, Express.js, React, Next.js, PostgreSQL, MySQL,
Redis, Docker, GitLab CI/CD, Grafana, Datadog. Master's in Information Systems
Management from Universitas Gunadarma.

Open to Tech Lead, Lead Backend Engineer, and Solution Architect roles.
```
