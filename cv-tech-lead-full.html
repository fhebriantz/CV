How I Work

  • The contract goes down in writing first. Data model and API contract agreed with consuming teams before build starts, then documented in Postman and kept current.
  • Take the feedback early. My designs get challenged before build, and the cheapest moment to find a simpler approach is while nothing has been written yet.
  • Estimate against a design, not a feeling. I plan my own delivery from the design I wrote, which is why the estimates hold up.
  • Map the process before automating it. On both the ERP and Fieldtrack the first deliverable was an understanding of how people actually worked. Designs that skip this get rebuilt.
  • Ship with the pipeline and the dashboards already there. CI/CD and monitoring are part of the release, not a follow-up ticket.
